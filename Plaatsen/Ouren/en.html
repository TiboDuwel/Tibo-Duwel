Ouren en Weiswampach

This is Ouren and Weiswampach. Ouren is in Belgium and Weiswampach in Luxembuurg. Ouren is a beautiful village near Burg-Reuland (Province Liège) in the East Cantons in the Ardennes. A part of the pictures has been taken on the German side of the Our river. They mostly speak French and German but you'll hear Dutch as well.
