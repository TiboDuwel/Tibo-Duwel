N63/E46 Marche-En-Famenne - Liège

This is the N63, also known as part of the E46. The road runs from Marche-en-Famenne to Liège. The section between Marche and Nandrin is a dual-carriageway road (two lanes in each direction) separated by a central median. It does not have official expressway status, so agricultural traffic is permitted. The speed limit is 120 km/h. The road features several junctions without acceleration lanes and lacks hard shoulders, though there are designated parking areas. From Nandrin to Neupré, the road continues; at Neupré, the limit drops to 70 km/h, and the second lane sometimes disappears. There is also a central turn lane. An abandoned petrol station stands beside the road. Between Neupré and Gonhis, there is another 120 km/h section, while the limit in Gonhis is 70 km/h. After the built-up area, there is another 120 km/h section—this time with expressway status. The road eventually reaches Liège. It was originally constructed in 1665 and upgraded to a paved highway standard between 1824 and 1832. It was widened to a dual-carriageway in the 1970s. In 2023, the dangerous at-grade intersection in Tinlot was replaced by a roundabout. The interchange with the N66 was also replaced by a roundabout. The *Viaduc de Somme-Leuze* (Somme-Leuze Viaduct) and several other sections underwent renovation between 2023 and 2025. These photos cover only the stretch from Tinlot to Liège.
